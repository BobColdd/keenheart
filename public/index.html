HOW A JOB RUNS

Survey to sign-off, in four moves.

Every job — big or small, cabling or code — follows the same sequence, so nothing gets skipped and you always know what's next.

  1. 01

    Site survey

    We walk the premises, measure loads or camera lines, and note what's already there before we quote anything.

  2. 02

    Design & quote

    A written scope and fixed quote — parts, labour, and timeline — no surprise call-outs later.

  3. 03

    Install

    Our crew runs the cabling, mounts the hardware, and configures the software or systems on site.

  4. 04

    Test & handover

    Full test, a walkthrough of the finished system, and paperwork — certificates included where required.
